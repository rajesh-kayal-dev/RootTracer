Branches that keep the workflow honest
Connection not received → Stay in setup and show diagnostics.
Insufficient evidence → Explain what is missing; do not invent a diagnosis.
Ineligible recovery → Explain the failed safety check; do not enable retry.
Acknowledgement without an outcome event → Pending or uncertain; never label it verified.
